THE THINGS PEOPLE ACTUALLY ASK

Let’s clear a few things up.

Does a washroom trip count as a break?+

Yes—it is time away from the cushion. The sensor can detect occupancy, not where you went. Return within 10 seconds to resume the same session; your away time is excluded either way.

Is 10 seconds a medical recommendation?+

No. It’s SitSync’s session-grouping rule. Your reminder interval is a separate preference. Neither setting establishes how long it is medically safe for you to sit.

Can one FSR measure my posture?+

No. A seat sensor cannot see your neck, spine, screen height, or whole-body alignment. One FSR estimates occupancy. It cannot measure side-to-side balance, posture, or body weight.

What if standing or walking isn’t an option?+

Choose movement or position changes that are comfortable and accessible to you. WHO includes wheeling among ways to be active. Seated movement is not captured by this prototype’s away-from-seat counter. WHO: physical activity ↗

TWO SEPARATE RULES

5 seconds to stop. 10 seconds to close.

The firmware stops an active vibration pattern after five continuous seconds below threshold. Python keeps a session open for ten seconds before saving it.

Motor status is inferred from commands; this firmware does not send motor feedback. The browser animation illustrates the pattern.
